# Questmates

**Questing with friends in WoW Forever, without the "wait, which quest are you on?"**

Everyone in the party with Questmates sees what everyone else is doing, how to catch up, and where to go next.

## Features

- **Party tracker**: each party member's current quest, objectives and progress, and what it means for you (*you have it too*, *you need this – 2 steps to catch up*, **Join**).
- **Catch up on any quest**: for a quest your friend has, see every earlier quest you need, in order, with the quest giver and distance. **Take me there** opens the map with a spotlight and route arrows, and puts the game's waypoint arrow on your screen.
- **Smart about prerequisites**: knows the WoW Forever quest chains, skips what you've done or have, and notices when a quest giver doesn't offer you a quest. It then works out what probably unlocks it.
- **Dungeons**: every WoW Forever dungeon and raid with its quests (including Krol'dok Stronghold, The Hall of Thanes, Ruins of Lordaeron…). Press **Get ready** and the whole party gets a numbered plan, nearest step first. Quests are shared and accepted automatically.
- **Show a quest to your friend**: it pops up on their screen with the whole chain.
- **Share where you're going**: your map pin shows on their map.
- **World map markers** with a legend, **party notes** styled like the game's own alerts, and **tooltips** showing your friends' kill counts on quest mobs.

## Use

`/qm` opens the Party Quest Journal · `/qm demo` shows a preview with a pretend party member · `/qm dungeons` lists dungeons · `/qm why <quest>` explains any quest's plan.

Everyone in the party needs Questmates.
